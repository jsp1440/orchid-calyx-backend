### Key Findings

1. **ProQuest Dissertations & Theses Global (PQDT) is the largest curated ETD corpus (5.5M+ citations / 3M+ full-text) but is fully subscription/paywalled with no open API or bulk harvest channel.** Official ProQuest FAQ: "PQDT Global now offers abstracts and indexing for over 5.5 million dissertations and theses, with full text (PDFs) for over 3 million of those works… over 4,100 institutions… more than 60 countries… Nearly 60% of new content added annually is from institutions outside of the U.S."[^2^] Growth ≈250,000 works/year.[^1^] The only sanctioned machine-scale channel is **ProQuest TDM Studio** (cloud workbench over licensed content, incl. dissertations; derived-data export only, no raw full-text dump).[^6^][^8^]
2. **NDLTD's Global ETD Search (~6.4–6.5M metadata records) is currently OFFLINE ("under redevelopment", HTTP 503 as of 2026-07-21)** — but the underlying **NDLTD Union Archive (ndltdunion.cs.uct.ac.za) is live, shows 6,536,154 records with per-collection statistics, and re-exposes merged metadata via OAI-PMH** — i.e., bulk metadata acquisition from NDLTD remains possible even while the search UI is down.[^13^][^14^][^15^]
3. **OATD is the largest open-access ETD index (~7.46M records from 1,100+ institutions), metadata harvested via OAI-PMH from source repositories — but OATD offers no public API/dump, indexes only first ~30 pages for snippets, full text stays at source repositories, and the site sits behind aggressive Cloudflare bot-screening** ("OATD is dealing with a number of misbehaved crawlers and robots"). Best path: harvest OATD's *source* repositories directly via their own OAI-PMH endpoints rather than scraping OATD.[^18^][^19^][^20^]
4. **EBSCO Open Dissertations (opendissertations.org) is free/open web, ~1.4M+ records from 320+ universities, built on OAI-harvested metadata with links back to institutional repositories;** incorporates American Doctoral Dissertations (1933–1955 print index, ~100k records) plus 1955–present extension.[^21^][^22^][^23^][^24^]
5. **HathiTrust offers the most machine-friendly bulk channels (HathiFiles daily/monthly metadata files; Bibliographic API; OAI feed for public-domain MARC; HTRC Extracted Features dataset of 18.7M volumes via rsync with no auth; Data Capsule for OCR)** — dissertations are a real but unquantified slice (e.g., ~24,000 UC Davis theses digitized by Google in 2017 alone), mostly in-copyright post-1920s.[^25^][^26^][^27^][^29^][^30^]
6. **Theses Canada (Library and Archives Canada) is a functioning national harvest hub: ~425,000 bibliographic records from ~70 universities, 217,000+ full-text PDFs, monthly OAI harvesting from university repositories** — the cleanest national-scale automated ingestion target in the Americas.[^31^][^32^][^34^]
7. **US consortial/state hubs punch above their weight: OhioLINK ETD Center (100,000+ open-access ETDs, 36 institutions, OAI-PMH feed, CC-licensed records)** and **eScholarship (73,000+ UC theses, systemwide OA ETD policy)** are prime bulk targets; Digital Commons Network aggregates 700+ bepress institutions (4M+ items incl. ETDs).[^36^][^37^][^40^][^41^][^39^]
8. **LA Referencia is the key Latin American harvest gateway: 9–11 national nodes, ~4.65M documents (~370 institutions), document mix historically ~33% master's + ~11% doctoral theses, built on OAI-PMH with an open-source harvester stack (LRHarvester/LRProvider), portal content under CC BY 4.0** — strategically the highest-orchid-relevance aggregator (Mexico, Costa Rica, Colombia, Ecuador, Peru nodes).[^43^][^44^][^45^][^46^]
9. **WorldCat Dissertations and Theses (OCLC) is the largest bibliographic thesis index (officially 26M+ records, updated daily) but subscription-only (FirstSearch/WorldCat APIs) with citations-only;** its free sibling OAIster aggregates 30M+ OA records (1,500+ contributors) and overlaps ETD metadata.[^48^][^50^]
10. **Google Scholar "harvests theses and dissertations from virtually all registered open-access repositories" but has no API and prohibits scraping** — useful as a discovery/QA backstop, not an acquisition channel.[^11^]

#### 1. ProQuest Dissertations & Theses Global (PQDT Global) — Confidence: HIGH
1. **Name:** ProQuest Dissertations & Theses Global (PQDT Global)
2. **Organization:** ProQuest (Clarivate), Ann Arbor, MI; "official repository of the Library of Congress" for US dissertations.[^7^]
3. **Geographic:** Global; 4,100+ institutions, 60+ countries; historically US/Canada-weighted, now ~60% of annual additions non-US.[^2^][^4^]
4. **Subject:** All disciplines incl. botany/plant sciences (ProQuest subject categories); citations from 1637, full-text mainly 1997+.[^2^]
5. **Count:** 5M+ citations & ~3M full-text per product page ("over 5 million citations and 3 million full text works");[^1^] 5.5M+ citations / 3M+ full-text per official FAQ;[^2^] some library pages cite "6 million entries… 3.6 million full text" (see Controversies).[^5^] +250k/year.[^1^]
6. **Access:** Restricted — institutional subscription; per-copy purchase via Dissertations Express (historical pricing $39–$101/copy, 2008 figure — stale).[^3^][^12^]
7. **Metadata:** Rich, editorially curated ("Every title is manually checked for completeness and accuracy");[^7^] citations + abstracts (doctoral since 1980, master's since 1988); 24-page previews for most post-1997 works.[^3^]
8. **Full text:** 3M+ PDFs to subscribers; OA-published items free via PQDT Open (profile #2).[^2^]
9. **API:** No public search/metadata API. TDM access only via TDM Studio (profile #3). PQDT Citation Index also on Web of Science (subscription).[^1^]
10. **OAI-PMH:** Not exposed for PQDT. (ProQuest hosts some institutional ETD collections, e.g. UNL's "Dissertations from ProQuest-UMI," managed separately.)[^39^]
11. **DOI/Handle:** ProQuest document URLs/IDs (dispub numbers); no DOI minting for most records.
12. **Bulk metadata harvest:** Not available openly; institutional customers may negotiate MARC record deliveries for their own holdings.
13. **Bulk download:** Prohibited — license terms bar systematic downloading ("Any systematically over-downloading of full-text documents from library-subscribed databases is prohibited" — typical library license language).[^12^]
14. **Rate limits:** Unknown (proprietary platform).
15. **Authentication:** Institutional SSO/IP (OpenAthens/Shibboleth); TDM Studio requires institution email.[^8^]
16. **Licensing:** Proprietary; authors retain copyright, ProQuest holds distribution rights.
17. **ToU:** Research use for authorized users; redistribution prohibited.[^12^]
18. **TDM:** Permitted via TDM Studio on licensed PQDT content; no raw corpus export (derived data only).[^6^][^8^]
19. **Copyright:** Author-held copyright; embargoes honored; some authors restrict.
20. **Preferred acquisition:** License PQDT + TDM Studio workbench; or extract OpenAlex/CORE cross-references and obtain full text from institutional repositories instead.
21. **Botanical relevance:** HIGH — largest source of US/Canadian botany, plant sciences, ecology, taxonomy theses; includes major orchid-relevant R1 programs.

### Trends & Signals

1. **Consolidation of thesis discovery into general OA aggregators** — the Theses.fr linked-data study documents DART-Europe's shutdown (3 Feb 2025) and frames it as "a broader shift from specialized thesis portals toward general open-access aggregators such as BASE, CORE, and NDLTD's Global ETD Search."[^55^] Implication: Calyx should treat CORE/OpenAlex as first-class ETD channels, not just ETD-specific silos.
2. **NDLTD infrastructure fragility** — Global ETD Search is 503 "under redevelopment" (observed 2026-07-21), while the UCT-hosted Union Archive remains live and freshly crawled (2026-07-15) with submission dates to Feb 2025.[^14^][^13^] Treat NDLTD as an unreliable-but-valuable partner; mirror what you harvest.
3. **Crawler-hostility is rising at open indexes** — OATD shows Cloudflare human-verification and a standing notice about "misbehaved crawlers and robots";[^18^] PQDT Open origin returned 521 on check date.[^10^] Automated ingestion should target OAI-PMH endpoints (protocol-sanctioned) rather than HTML scraping.
4. **OA-by-default ETD policies spreading** — UC systemwide Open Access for Theses and Dissertations policy (default OA, author-retained copyright, optional CC),[^59^] OhioLINK 3.0 with batch publish + MARC-from-OAI,[^36^] LAC monthly harvesting at national scale.[^32^] The harvestable OA-ETD pool keeps growing.
5. **Retrospective opening of legacy dissertations** — UCSF added 3,000+ 1965–2006 theses to eScholarship;[^41b^] UC Davis opened 1,047 Google-digitized theses in HathiTrust via rights-holder CC declarations.[^30^] Historic botanical theses increasingly reachable.
6. **Citation indexing of ETDs** — ProQuest D&T Citation Index on Web of Science (>5.5M works) normalizes ETDs as citable, linked literature.[^1^]
7. **ETD growth velocity** — PQDT +250k works/yr;[^1^] OATD grew from ~3.5M (FAQ-era) to 7.46M;[^18^][^19^] NDLTD union from ~4M (2015 launch) to 6.5M (2024–25).[^13^][^57^]
8. **Licensing heterogeneity persists** — OhioLINK works commonly CC BY-NC-ND;[^37^] eScholarship default all-rights-reserved;[^59^] OATD explicitly defers to local statements.[^19^] Calyx needs per-record license capture at harvest time.

### Controversies & Conflicting Claims

1. **PQDT size figures diverge**: official product page "over 5 million citations and 3 million full text";[^1^] official FAQ "over 5.5 million… over 3 million";[^2^] Cornell relaying ProQuest: "more than 6 million entries… more than 3.6 million full text";[^5^] Cochrane (Nov 2021) "more than 5 million… full text… over 2.5 million."[^52^] Treat counts as marketing-fuzzy; verify via in-database year>1000 query trick (ProQuest's own suggestion).[^2^]
2. **OATD count drift**: homepage 7,464,811 (2026) vs. FAQ text "over 3.5 million" (never updated) vs. 2025 libguide "7.5 million."[^18^][^19^][^20^] Homepage counter is authoritative.
3. **WorldCat Dissertations count**: OCLC official help says "26,000,000+ records";[^48^] library guides say 5M/8M;[^48b^] a 2024 study table lists WorldCat (metadata) at 11.2M.[^16^] Likely scope differences (thesis-only vs. thesis-derived publications). Flag for Phase 3.
4. **"Largest OA ETD collection" claims**: OhioLINK cites Google Scholar (2019) calling its ETD Center the world's largest OA ETD collection,[^36^] while OATD (7.46M) and NDLTD (6.5M) are far larger by records — different metrics (full-text-hosted vs. index).
5. **NDLTD record totals**: 6,486,347 (Jun 2024), 6,447,277 (Dec 2024 paper), 6,536,154 (union stats page, 2025–26) — dedupe/update cycles cause non-monotonic counts.[^15^][^16^][^13^]
6. **PQDT Open vitality**: described as growing ("we expect to have many more open access dissertations") yet site returned 521 on 2026-07-21; third-party 2025 citations still link its PDFs.[^9^][^10^] Status genuinely uncertain — monitor.
7. **LA Referencia Mexico node**: portal lists México (REMERI) while a 2021 policy paper notes "México dejó de ser un nodo activo a finales de 2020."[^43^][^46^] Node status ambiguous.

### Recommended Deep-Dive Areas (Phase 3)

1. **NDLTD Union Archive OAI-PMH endpoint verification** — confirm baseURL(s) (union.ndltd.org vs. ndltdunion.cs.uct.ac.za), available metadataPrefixes (oai_dc, oai_etdms), set structure, harvest cadence, and whether the 503 redevelopment changes the data-provider endpoint. Highest-value single action.
2. **OATD source-repository enumeration** — obtain OATD's contributing-sites list, extract the OAI baseURLs of the ~1,100 sources, and dedupe against NDLTD/CORE registries to build Calyx's master OAI target list. (OATD FAQ confirms OAI-sourcing and points to opendoar/openarchives registration.)[^19^]
3. **ProQuest TDM Studio feasibility study** — cost/licensing for PQDT workbench; confirm classterm taxonomy includes botany/plant-science categories; test derived-metadata export limits; assess whether a Calyx-affiliated university license is obtainable.[^7^][^8^]
4. **LA Referencia LRProvider harvesting** — validate the re-exposure OAI endpoint, thesis-set queries (tesis de doctorado/maestría), per-node coverage of botanical institutions (UNAM, UCR, Universidad de los Andes, San Marcos, PUCP), and node-level alternatives (REMERI, Alicia) given the Mexico-node ambiguity.[^43^][^46^]
5. **HathiTrust thesis subset profiling** — use HathiFiles genre/format fields to enumerate dissertation volumes, split PD vs. in-copyright, and prototype EF-dataset rsync for the PD subset (historic botany theses).[^26^][^30^]
6. **Theses Canada bulk mechanics** — confirm whether LAC exposes its own OAI endpoint or only via NDLTD; quantify the ProQuest 4-year-lag subset; test PDF fetch at scale.[^32^][^34^]
7. **License-aware ingestion spec** — design per-record license capture (CC vs. all-rights-reserved vs. unknown) since TDM rights hinge on it (OhioLINK CC BY-NC-ND examples, eScholarship defaults, OATD local-priority rule).[^19^][^37^][^59^]
8. **PQDT Open health + EBSCO OD bulk options** — re-probe pqdtopen.proquest.com; ask EBSCO (M. Richter contact) whether metadata subscribers can obtain record dumps.[^10^][^22^]
9. **Botanical-yield benchmarking** — run identical orchid query sets ("Orchidaceae", "orchid", "Cattleya", "Vanilla") across OpenAlex, NDLTD union, OATD, LA Referencia, OhioLINK, eScholarship to rank sources by yield/dedupe cost before committing harvest order.
10. **WorldCat Dissertations count reconciliation + OAIster overlap** — resolve the 5M/11M/26M discrepancy and measure OAIster∩NDLTD∩CORE overlap to avoid duplicate pipelines.[^48^][^50^]
