# Priority Batch — BUILD-KE-308 through BUILD-BRAIN-119

Status: candidate implementation; draft only.

## Implemented vertical slices

1. **BUILD-KE-308** — deterministic integrated-glossary coverage auditing across definitions, evidence, accessible media, and related concepts.
2. **BUILD-ATLAS-409** — evidence-linked restoration-site ranking using habitat suitability, conservation priority, feasibility, and threat-reduction potential.
3. **BUILD-RS-506** — deterministic data-quality audit manifests covering completeness, validity, consistency, provenance, and licensing.
4. **BUILD-CON-606** — candidate environmental alerts for temperature, humidity, light, and moisture readings outside configured ranges.
5. **BUILD-MATRIX-706** — deterministic character information-gain ranking for identification workflows.
6. **BUILD-VISION-806** — confidence-ranked candidate observations for mechanical, pest, disease, nutritional, environmental, or unknown damage.
7. **BUILD-PUB-906** — evidence-backed article assembly with deterministic ordering and publication disabled.
8. **BUILD-INT-957** — bounded exponential retry policy with maximum attempts and maximum delay.
9. **BUILD-MC-207** — chronological portfolio trend comparison for admitted, running, blocked, and completed work.
10. **BUILD-BRAIN-119** — direct and transitive architecture dependency-impact analysis.

## Validation coverage

Focused tests cover glossary completeness, deterministic restoration ranking, duplicate quality checks, environmental thresholds, information-gain ordering, duplicate damage observations, publication safeguards, retry bounds, chronological portfolio snapshots, and transitive impact analysis.

## Safety boundary

These contracts audit, rank, stage, or explain candidate work. They do not approve restoration actions, issue cultivation or medical diagnoses, publish articles, activate external retries, mutate production records, merge code, deploy services, or mutate the production Knowledge Graph.
